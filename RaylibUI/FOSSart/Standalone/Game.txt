@ABOUT
@title=About rhYciv
@width=460
@button=Ok
Version %STRING0
^
rhYciv is an open reimplementation of the turn-based strategy game Civilization
II, built on the axx0/Civ2-clone engine and shipped with its own rules, art and
text so that it runs on nothing but itself.
^
Released under the GPL. The bundled ruleset derives from Freeciv; the art and
the written material are the project's own. Full attribution for every shipped
file is in ASSET-MANIFEST.tsv, and the licences are in LICENSE and NOTICE.md.
