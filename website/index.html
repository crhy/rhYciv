A familiar foundation, deliberately modernized

The part you loved.
Without the baggage.

rhYciv focuses on the fast, readable empire-building loop that made classic turn-based strategy so compelling: explore, settle, research, build, negotiate, fight, expand.

Advisors, the throne room, and spaceship victory are deliberately outside the core scope. Conquest stays front and center while the renderer, controls, packaging, testing, and open art pipeline move forward.

Why rhYciv

Classic strategy,
modern expectations.

The current beta runs standalone, renders cleanly on high-DPI displays, adds modern map controls, and ships with an increasingly complete FOSS art set.

01

Standalone and open

Bundled rules, city names, interface atlases, terrain, units, cities, citizens, and icons mean the current beta no longer needs a commercial Civilization II installation.

02

Sharp at modern resolutions

A stable 1920×1080 logical canvas scales to high-resolution displays while fonts, map backing, vector elements, and FOSS art render at native density.

03

Better map control

Zoom, pan, center, route previews, quick tile information, group movement, display tuning, and improved mouse interactions.

04

Modern build quality

.NET 9, automated tests, a quality gate, sandboxed Flatpak packaging, and clean-profile startup give the project a maintainable base.

05

Art that can evolve

Replacement art is part of the project itself, so units, terrain, citizens, icons, advances, and other assets can keep improving without relying on proprietary originals.
