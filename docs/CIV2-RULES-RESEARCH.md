# Civ II rules research

## #161 — How many barbarians come out of a village (goody hut)?

**Question.** Issue #161 concerns the size of the barbarian horde spawned when a unit
enters a village, currently `Math.Max(2, Math.Min(3, game.BarbarianActivity + 1))`
in `Engine/src/UnitActions/MovementFunctions.cs` (VillagesOnly/RovingBands -> 2,
RestlessTribes/RagingHordes -> 3). The claimed rule is "Civ II hut hordes are a
pair, scaling to 3 on Restless/Raging".

**Sources checked.**

1. Local wiki copy `.civ2-wiki/Village_(Civ2).wiki`: "The village spawns a
   barbarian unit in every adjacent unoccupied land tile." (up to 6 in the hex
   grid; the page cites the Civ II Instruction Manual only for flavour text, not
   for the count).
2. William Keenan, "The Barbarian Paper" v1.2, Scenario League Wiki
   (https://sleague.civfanatics.com/index.php?title=The_Barbarian_Paper), also
   archived at https://coc.apolytonarchive.net/guides/barbarian_paper.shtml:
   "Hostile barbarian hordes appear in the squares surrounding the goody hut and
   can number from one unit up to as many as eight units." Its Barbarian Activity
   table ties activity level to uprising *frequency*, rebel-band *size* and
   leader ransom value ("Villages Only: ... Never"; "Roving Bands: Half Normal,
   Small" ...), not to the number of units a hut releases.
3. Apolyton forum, Scenario League / Civ2-Creation archive, "Goody Hut
   limitations and usefulness"
   (https://apolyton.net/forum/miscellaneous/archives/scenario-league-civ2-creation-archive/81005-goody-hut-limitations-and-usefulness):
   "The Quantity of Barbarians generated by a hut is always 1 or 8 ... You will
   always get 1 Barb until at some point the huts begin to generate 8 - and from
   then on it never goes back to 1."

**Agreement.** Hut hordes come from the tiles adjacent to the hut; the count is
not a fixed 2-or-3 pair, and no source links it to the four Barbarian Activity
settings.

**Unverified / contradictory.** The three sources disagree on the number: every
adjacent unoccupied land tile (<= 6), "one ... up to as many as eight", or
strictly "1 or 8". No Civ II source supports the "at least 2, 3 on
Restless/Raging" rule currently in the code, nor the `PartySize` 2/3/4 used by
`Engine/src/BarbarianUprisings.cs` (the Barbarian Paper says rebel bands "start
off with two units then grow in size over time", tied to game turn and
technology, with activity setting small/medium/large bands).

**Conclusion.** Sources disagree and nothing conclusive was found, so per the
research protocol no game-code change was made and no GitHub comment posted.
The existing `Math.Max(2, Math.Min(3, BarbarianActivity + 1))` in master is an
unverified approximation of a rule that real Civ II most likely implements as
"1 barbarian, later 8". No docs/CIV2-*.md file currently states the hut-horde
rule, so nothing needed correcting.
